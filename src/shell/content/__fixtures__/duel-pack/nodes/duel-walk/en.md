# Unpredictable walk

## primer
### card 1
Change direction without warning and reward when {name} looks at you.

## checkpoint
### tq1
{name} pulls toward a smell. What do you do?
- [x] Turn and walk the other way
- [ ] Pull on the leash
- [ ] Repeat its name
- why: A calm turn makes the walk a two-way thing again.

### tq2
Which handler keeps {name} paying attention?
- [ ] The handler walks looking at the phone
- [ ] The handler turns and looks at {name}
- why: Turning and looking invites {name} to check in with you.
