# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines how the illustrations of Lessons and Primer cards are produced, bundled and checked, so every picture is on-style, available offline and teaches something specific rather than decorating.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bundled Lesson illustrations
The system SHALL ship every Lesson and Primer illustration inside the app bundle and SHALL draw it without any network request.

#### Scenario: Offline Lesson
- **WHEN** the device is offline and a theory Lesson is opened
- **THEN** every illustration of the Lesson is drawn and none shows the placeholder

#### Scenario: Unknown art name
- **WHEN** an image block names art that is not bundled
- **THEN** the themed placeholder is shown and the Lesson remains readable and completable

### Requirement: One visual style
The system SHALL draw every illustration in the design system's flat paper-cut style: the shared palette, ink outlines, the mascot Dog's proportions and coat, and the same Person figure, on a 16:9 canvas.

#### Scenario: Consistent cast
- **WHEN** two Lessons both show the Dog and its Person
- **THEN** both use the same Dog and Person figures and palette

### Requirement: Illustrations teach one idea
Each illustration SHALL show one specific idea from its Lesson (a body signal, a leash shape, a position, a sequence or a do / don't contrast) and its caption SHALL name that idea; no illustration is decorative only.

#### Scenario: Leash shape diagram
- **WHEN** a Lesson explains the slack "U" of the leash
- **THEN** its illustration shows the slack U next to a taut leash and the caption names the difference

#### Scenario: Do / don't pair
- **WHEN** a Lesson shows a common mistake
- **THEN** the right and wrong versions appear side by side, told apart by a check and a cross icon, not by colour alone

### Requirement: Real captures for How-to-play
The system SHALL illustrate each control of a How-to-play Lesson with a capture of the real game screen, annotated to point at the control it describes.

#### Scenario: Control capture
- **WHEN** a How-to-play Lesson describes the joystick
- **THEN** its image is a capture of the walking screen with the joystick highlighted

#### Scenario: Controls change
- **WHEN** a control's appearance changes in the game
- **THEN** the captures can be regenerated by one script without hand editing

### Requirement: Readable at phone width
Each illustration SHALL stay legible at 320 points wide, SHALL contain no words (meaning is carried by shapes, arrows and check / cross icons, and words live in the localized caption), and SHALL provide alt text describing what it shows. How-to-play captures are exempt from the no-words rule because they show the real game screen.

#### Scenario: Narrow phone
- **WHEN** an illustration is shown at 320 points wide
- **THEN** its key shapes and icons are distinguishable and nothing is clipped

#### Scenario: Another language
- **WHEN** the app runs in a language other than Spanish
- **THEN** no theory illustration shows Spanish words

#### Scenario: Screen reader
- **WHEN** a screen reader reaches an illustration
- **THEN** it announces the alt text and the caption
