# Proposal

## Why

Retention and content breadth come last: game feel, daily habit loop, and the world map. Phase 4 of 4.

## What Changes

- Require a scenery set with a minimum density for every scenario so no world ships as a bare route (playtest finding).
- Add the Sintonía sync combo, the Go Sniff reward (World 2+), Walk Diary with daily streak, "El Paseo del Día", and the 4-world progression map (World 1-2 seeded, World 3-4 stubbed).
- Add Dog reactivity: staged visible and audible reactions (alert, barking and lunging) driven by arousal.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `sync-combo`: Sintonía feedback.
- `go-sniff`: Contextual stress-relief reward.
- `walk-diary-streak`: One-tap real-world check-in and streak.
- `daily-walk`: Procedurally generated daily level.
- `world-progression`: Four-world mechanics map and unlocks.
- `dog-reactivity`: Staged reactions (alert, barking, lunging) driven by arousal.

### Modified Capabilities

(none)

## Impact

- Depends on `onboarding-and-accounts`. `dog-reactivity` depends on the arousal model in `training-loop-and-learning` and introduces the first audio (a Non-Goal in `base-leadmate`). Design and tasks are still to be written.
