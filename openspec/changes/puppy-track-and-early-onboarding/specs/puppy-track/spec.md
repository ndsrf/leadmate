# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Provides puppy-specific learning modules addressing the critical developmental window under six months of age, focusing on bite inhibition, graduated domestic sound habituation, and calm veterinary handling.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Puppy bite inhibition module
The puppy track SHALL provide a targeted learning module on puppy mouthing and bite inhibition. It SHALL explain that mouthing is exploratory behavior driven by teething and play arousal, establish that human hands and clothing are not toys, and instruct the handler in redirecting to chew toys and using calm reverse timeouts without physical correction.

#### Scenario: Bite inhibition guidance
- **WHEN** the user opens the bite inhibition module
- **THEN** it teaches redirecting needle teeth to toys, keeping hands calm, and stepping away if the puppy escalates, with zero scolding or physical punishment

#### Scenario: Trade-up protocol for puppies
- **WHEN** the puppy takes a forbidden household object
- **THEN** the module instructs the user to offer a high-value toy or treat trade rather than prying the puppy's jaws open

### Requirement: Domestic sound desensitisation module
The puppy track SHALL provide a graduated sound habituation module covering common domestic auditory triggers, including vacuum cleaners, doorbells, and thunder/fireworks. It SHALL instruct the handler to expose the puppy at low, sub-threshold volumes using household or recorded sounds while pairing the sound with calm rest and high-value rewards.

#### Scenario: Sub-threshold sound habituation guidance
- **WHEN** the user opens the sound habituation module
- **THEN** it provides step-by-step guidance on playing domestic trigger sounds at controlled, low volumes and directs the handler to reward calm composure

#### Scenario: Reaction threshold handling
- **WHEN** the puppy shows alert or startled body language during sound exposure
- **THEN** the module instructs the handler to lower the volume immediately and increase distance until the puppy regains calm

### Requirement: Gentle body handling and vet prep module
The puppy track SHALL provide a cooperative care module preparing the puppy for grooming and veterinary examinations. It SHALL guide handlers through sequential touch exercises for paws, ears, eyes, and mouth, pairing brief, gentle contact with calm marking and rewards.

#### Scenario: Cooperative touch sequence
- **WHEN** the user conducts a handling drill
- **THEN** the exercise guides touching a single paw or ear for two seconds, immediately marking with calm praise and delivering a reward

#### Scenario: Low-stress veterinary acclimation
- **WHEN** the user reviews the vet preparation checklist
- **THEN** it emphasizes mock table examinations, muzzle touch acclimation, and keeping early veterinary visits positive and reward-rich
