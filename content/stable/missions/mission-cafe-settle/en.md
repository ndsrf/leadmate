# A Coffee in Calm

## target
Have a whole coffee on a terrace with {name} on the mat. Never pay begging: wait, and mark and reward only when the Dog lies down by choice.

## success
{name} lies on the mat, stays on it for most of the coffee, and gets nothing from the table.

## problems
### begs
- label: Begs for food from the table
- reassure: This is very normal: the table smells of food and, at home, begging may have worked once.
- cause: Begging still pays: a look, a word or a bite has confirmed that insisting works.
- c1: Do not look, do not talk and do not hand over anything from the table. Wait, and pay only when the Dog lies down by choice.
- c2: Do not give in at the end: giving in on the tenth try teaches trying eleven times.
- easier: The Dog lies on the mat without getting anything from the table, 2 times.
- alt: The Dog lies on the mat while the Handler drops a treat between the paws
- caption: Calm is paid, not begging

### up
- label: Gets up every time someone passes
- reassure: Watching what moves is natural. Getting up at first is common on a terrace.
- cause: The table is probably too close to the walkway, or the Dog arrived with energy still unspent.
- c1: Move to a corner by the wall and pay a small treat each time the Dog lies down again.
- c2: If the Dog gets up more than three times in a row, shorten the visit and leave while it still settles.
- easier: The Dog lies down again after watching someone pass, 2 times.
- alt: A waiter passes in the distance while the Dog stays lying on the mat
- caption: Watching without getting up is already an achievement

### chew
- label: Does not want the chew
- reassure: In a new place it is common not to feel like chewing at first.
- cause: There may be too much that is new, or the chew may not be the one the Dog likes best.
- c1: Offer the Dog's favourite chew at home first, so it comes to go with the mat.
- c2: If the Dog does not want it, do not force it: try a small treat on the mat when it lies down.
- easier: The Dog chews on the mat at home, 3 times.
- alt: The Dog chews a chew toy lying on the mat
- caption: Something good to do on the mat

## general
- reassure: A coffee with ups and downs is not a failure. Every time you do not pay begging, it counts.
- cause: The most common mistake is giving in without noticing: a bite, a look, a few words.
- c1: For one coffee, focus only on not responding to begging.
- c2: Review the "Settling at the Table" lesson.
- easier: Pay calm just once during the visit.
- alt: The Handler drinks a coffee while the Dog rests on its mat
- caption: A coffee without begging
