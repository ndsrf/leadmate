# The Busy Park

## target
Walk for 10 minutes through an active park: handle fast movers first, ground food next, with decompression sniffing in between.

## success
Complete the walk without any Rigid Pull.

## problems
### overwhelmed
- label: Became overwhelmed
- reassure: A bustling park carries heavy sensory load. Trigger stacking is not a failure.
- cause: Distractors stacked up without decompression breaks in between.
- c1: Build in a sniffing break every few minutes.
- c2: Exit the park perimeter temporarily if arousal spikes.
- easier: Five minutes along the outer edge of the park.
- alt: A Dog sniffs calmly in a quiet corner of the park
- caption: Decompression sniffing breaks

### food
- label: Found discarded food
- reassure: Public parks are littered with scraps. It happens to every Handler.
- cause: Attention was locked on moving Distractors, missing ground hazards.
- c1: Survey the area before paying out leash length.
- c2: Deliver the "Leave It" Cue before the Dog closes the distance.
- easier: Pass discarded food using "Leave It" in the park, 1 time.
- alt: A Handler inspects the grass before giving leash length
- caption: Scan the ground

### me
- label: I felt overwhelmed
- reassure: Feeling stressed is human. Handler regulation takes practice too.
- cause: You tried to manage all environmental factors simultaneously.
- c1: Breathe and pause: the Poste technique anchors your composure too.
- c2: Address one Distractor at a time.
- easier: A short stroll through the park at an off-peak hour.
- alt: A Handler takes a deep breath while standing with the calm Dog
- caption: Breathe and anchor

## general
- reassure: You have made significant progress. A busy park is the ultimate real-world test.
- cause: Venturing out during maximum foot-traffic hours is common.
- c1: Start by visiting during calm hours.
- c2: Review the busy park lesson.
- easier: Five minutes in a park with light activity.
- alt: An early morning park scene with minimal activity
- caption: Off-peak hours
