# Joggers in the Park

## target
On a long leash, perform Leash Gathering and stop when a jogger passes.

## success
The jogger passes and your Dog does not reach the end of the leash.

## problems
### chases
- label: Chases after the jogger
- reassure: Chasing fast-moving targets is pure predatory motor pattern. It can be managed.
- cause: Too much Slack remained available when the jogger sprinted past.
- c1: Perform Leash Gathering as soon as you spot a jogger in the distance.
- c2: Reward when the Dog notices the jogger and voluntarily checks back with you.
- easier: Perform Leash Gathering upon spotting a distant jogger, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler performs Leash Gathering upon spotting a runner
- caption: Leash Gathering in time

### tangle
- label: The leash gets tangled
- reassure: Long lines tangle for everyone early on.
- cause: Handling 3 meters of leash requires muscle memory and coordination.
- c1: Hold excess line in loose, accordion folds, never wrapped around hands.
- c2: Practice feeding and Leash Gathering indoors without the Dog.
- easier: Gather and pay out the long leash in an empty park, 3 times.
- alt: A hand holds the long line in loose, accordion folds
- caption: Loose folds

### surprise
- label: Appeared suddenly
- reassure: Runners approach silently. You did the right thing by trying.
- cause: Positioned in an area with restricted sightlines.
- c1: Walk through open meadows, well clear of paved running trails.
- c2: If caught off guard, stop and maintain Slack on the leash.
- easier: Watch a runner pass from an open area, 1 time.
- alt: An open area in the park situated far from the jogging path
- caption: Open areas

## general
- reassure: Parks offer high environmental arousal. Any incremental success is meaningful.
- cause: Visiting during peak runner hours is the common pitfall.
- c1: Pick off-peak hours with fewer park visitors.
- c2: Review the long line handling lesson.
- easier: Ten minutes on a long leash without any runners present.
- alt: A nearly empty park in the early morning
- caption: Fewer runners
