# Five Minutes with a Distractor

## target
Walk for 5 minutes with Slack in the leash while passing near a Distractor.

## success
Pass the Distractor without any Rigid Pull.

## problems
### fixates
- label: Fixates and will not listen
- reassure: Fixation on an object is completely natural canine behavior.
- cause: You were likely positioned too close to the Distractor.
- c1: Increase distance until the Dog is able to look at you.
- c2: Use the Marker the instant the Dog breaks eye contact with the Distractor.
- easier: Observe the Distractor from a distance and check in with you, 3 times.
- alt: A Dog looks at a distant bicycle and then checks in with the Handler
- caption: More distance

### pulls
- label: Pulls from the very start
- reassure: Pulling upon initial departure is common: anticipation and energy run high.
- cause: Initial walk excitement is still peaked.
- c1: Allow two minutes of decompression sniffing before asking for focus.
- c2: Execute a Poste on every pull, without exception.
- easier: Two minutes of Slack on a quiet street.
- alt: A Dog sniffs near the building entrance before the walk begins
- caption: Sniff first

### lunges
- label: Lunges toward the Distractor
- reassure: Lunging is startling, but improves steadily with distance and time.
- cause: The Distractor approached too closely or too rapidly.
- c1: Cross the street or turn away before the encounter gets close.
- c2: If lunging occurs, execute a Poste and wait for return, without scolding.
- easier: Pass a full sidewalk width away from the Distractor, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler crosses the street to yield space to an oncoming bicycle
- caption: Yield space

## general
- reassure: Real-world walks present unpredictable surprises. A difficult day does not undo training.
- cause: Venturing down a street with heavy traffic is the usual error.
- c1: Pick a quieter time and a lower-traffic street.
- c2: Keep the walk short and end on a positive note.
- easier: Three minutes of Slack in the leash without Distractors.
- alt: A quiet street early in the morning
- caption: A quiet street
