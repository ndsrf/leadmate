# Shields on Your Street

## target
When an e-scooter or bicycle passes, position a parked car as a Visual Shield between your Dog and the street.

## success
The vehicle passes without your Dog lunging.

## problems
### late
- label: Did not have enough time
- reassure: Scooters move very quickly. Everyone gets surprised occasionally.
- cause: You looked for a Visual Shield only once the vehicle was already nearby.
- c1: Walk proactively along parked cars on streets frequented by scooters.
- c2: Listen closely: many scooters can be heard before they are seen.
- easier: Walk alongside parked cars on a quiet street, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler walks close to a line of parked cars
- caption: Walk close to the shield

### sees
- label: Saw it anyway
- reassure: A shield does not block every angle. Making the tactical effort is great progress.
- cause: The car was too low or your Dog was walking on the outside flank.
- c1: Position your Dog on your inner side, closest to the parked cars.
- c2: Cue "Let's Go" before stepping out from behind the shield.
- easier: Cue "Let's Go" while positioned behind a parked car, 2 times.
- alt: The Dog walks on the side sheltered by parked cars
- caption: Dog on the sheltered side

### nocar
- label: No parked cars were available
- reassure: Physical shields are not always available. Alternative solutions exist.
- cause: The chosen street had no parked cars.
- c1: Use an entrance doorway, a tree, or Handler Shielding as visual cover.
- c2: Select an alternate street that features parked vehicles.
- easier: Use Handler Shielding between your Dog and the road when traffic passes, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler steps between the Dog and the roadway
- caption: Handler Shielding

## general
- reassure: Sidewalks with scooter traffic present heavy environmental demands.
- cause: Choosing a street that is too congested is the common mistake.
- c1: Start on a street with light, slow traffic.
- c2: Review the Visual Shields lesson.
- easier: Use a shield with a slow bicycle, 1 time.
- alt: A quiet residential street lined with parked cars
- caption: A quiet street
