# A Quiet Home

## target
When demanding attention inside the home, calmly redirect: step toward its space without looking, talking, or touching. Keep high-arousal play outdoors.

## success
Yields space and heads to rest in its own bed independently.

## problems
### insists
- label: Persists and will not move away
- reassure: Expected reaction: if demanding previously succeeded, persistence initially spikes. It is an extinction burst showing the change is noticed.
- cause: Subtle feedback is likely still leaking: eye contact, a word, or a concession pet at the end.
- c1: Avoid eye contact and calmly claim space by walking toward the Dog until it yields completely.
- c2: Do not give in: conceding on the tenth attempt teaches trying eleven times.
- easier: Step back two paces as you walk toward its space, 3 times.
- alt: The Handler calmly steps toward the Dog with arms down, and the Dog yields space
- caption: Claim space without touch

### barks
- label: Demand barks at me
- reassure: Demand barking frequently emerges when earlier attention-seeking strategies stop working.
- cause: Barking previously unlocked interaction, prompting a repeat attempt.
- c1: Do not react to barking, not even to ask for quiet: look away and wait out the noise.
- c2: When silent for several seconds, and only then, use your Marker softly.
- easier: Cease barking without receiving any reaction, 2 times.
- alt: The Dog looks in silence at the Handler, who stands facing away
- caption: Silence earns results

### comesback
- label: Leaves but returns immediately
- reassure: Returning right away is common: testing whether the boundary remains consistent.
- cause: Excess unspent physical or mental energy, or lacking a clear calm job at home.
- c1: Provide a decompression sniff walk and outdoor play before asking for household calm.
- c2: When settled on its bed, deliver a long-lasting chew to keep focus grounded.
- easier: Stay settled in bed for two minutes after yielding space, 2 times.
- alt: The Dog lies in bed chewing on a safe chew toy
- caption: An engaging activity in bed

## general
- reassure: A day full of persistent demands is not a failure. Every withheld reaction reinforces the rule.
- cause: Inadvertently feeding attention through passing glances or casual words is the usual culprit.
- c1: For one entire day, focus solely on withholding eye contact when attention is demanded.
- c2: Review the "Resting at Home" lesson.
- easier: Calmly claim space once per day.
- alt: The Handler reads on the couch while the Dog rests nearby
- caption: Avoid looking during demand behaviors
