# World 7 · Arriving at the Terrace

## primer
### card 1
Remember: arrive after a sniffing walk, on a slack leash, and pick the corner by the wall. If there is food on the ground, say "Leave It" once.
- caption: The corner by the wall is out of the waiters' path

### card 2
In the game, walk on a slack leash toward the benches and the kiosk at the end, and tap Leave It, the open hand, as soon as {name} looks at food on the ground.

## checkpoint
### tq1
There is a piece of food on the ground near the tables. What do you do?
- [x] Say "Leave It" once as soon as {name} sees it
- [ ] Rush over to take it out of the Dog's mouth
- [ ] Repeat "Leave It" louder until the Dog backs off
- why: Say it once, before the Dog reaches it, and reward from your hand when it turns toward you. Repeating it or rushing does not help the Dog get it right.

### tq2
Which table is better for {name}?
- [ ] The corner table by the wall, with {name} on the mat on the wall side
- [ ] A table by the waiters' aisle, with {name} in the middle of the passage and people crossing on both sides
- why: The corner has less movement and {name} is in nobody's way. In the aisle, everyone who passes is something new.

### tq3
How is it best to arrive at the terrace?
- [x] After a long sniffing walk, on a slack leash
- [ ] Straight from home, so the Dog does not have to wait
- [ ] After a game of fetch so the Dog is tired out
- why: A sniffing walk lets energy out and lowers excitement; arriving in a rush or already wound up makes it harder. And always ask the venue whether dogs are welcome.
