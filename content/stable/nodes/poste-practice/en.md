# Practice: The Poste

## primer
### card 1
Remember: when the leash goes Taut, stop like a Poste until it returns to Slack.
- caption: How to stop like a Poste when the Dog pulls

## checkpoint
### tq1
What does your Dog learn if you keep moving forward while it pulls?
- [x] That pulling gets it where it wants to go
- [ ] That it should walk by your side
- [ ] That pulling achieves nothing
- why: Every step you take with a Taut leash rewards the pull: pulling brings it closer to what it wants.

### tq2
What happens if you pull back?
- [x] It learns to pull even harder
- [ ] It learns to always walk by your side
- [ ] It loses interest in what it saw
- why: It is a reflex: if you pull backward, its body pushes in opposition and you both end up pulling harder.

### tq3
You use Poste. When do you resume walking?
- [x] As soon as the leash returns to Slack
- [ ] When you count to ten seconds
- [ ] When it stops looking around
- why: A Slack leash is what reopens the path forward; that way the Dog knows exactly what it did right.

## exercises
- poste: Outside: the next time your Dog pulls, stop like a Poste and count to five before moving.
- tension: At home: walk through the living room with a Slack leash and stop each time it goes Taut.
