# World 3 · The Slack U-Leash

## primer
### card 1
Remember: watch the shape of the leash. If the U closes into a J, slow down or say its name before the Dog pulls.
- caption: U, J, straight line: the J is the warning

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why is it best to act when you see the J shape?
- [x] Because the Dog is not yet locked onto the scent
- [ ] Because the leash is already completely Taut
- [ ] Because that lets you give more leash
- why: In the J shape the Dog can still hear you; once the leash is Taut, it is locked in and finding its way back is much harder.

### tq2
The U shortens into a J. What do you do?
- [x] Slow my pace or say its name once
- [ ] Keep walking the same until the leash goes Taut
- [ ] Repeat its name until it comes back
- why: As soon as you see the J, change something before tension arrives: slow your pace a bit or say its name once in a soft voice.

### tq3
Where do you hold your leash hand?
- [x] Steady, right by your waist
- [ ] Reaching forward, following the Dog
- [ ] Held up high, at chest level
- why: If you stretch your arm out, the U remains but your hand follows and the Dog learns to forge ahead: the U comes from proximity.

## exercises

- attention: Outside: change direction smoothly and verify that your Dog follows you.
- poste: Outside: after a startle, let the Dog sniff for a few seconds before continuing.
