## primer
### card 1
Remember: change direction without warning, keep the leash Slack, and reward every glance {name} gives you. Use "Let's Go" to set off together and "Go Sniff" to give them time to sniff.
- caption: Unexpected turns invite a Check-in

## checkpoint
### tq1
{name} walks ahead on autopilot and has not looked at you in a while. What helps most?
- [x] Turn without warning and let {name} find you
- [ ] Keep walking straight and chat to {name} the whole way
- [ ] Keep to the usual route, just a bit faster
- why: An unexpected turn makes {name} look for you, and that glance is a Check-in you can reward. Constant chatter keeps {name} on autopilot.

### tq2
Which walk keeps {name} paying attention?
- [ ] The handler walks in a straight line while looking at a phone
- [ ] The handler turns and looks back at {name}
- why: Turning and looking invites {name} to look for you, so the choice to check in becomes {name}'s own.

### tq3
Which Cue do you use to release {name} to explore a safe patch of grass?
- [x] "Go Sniff"
- [ ] "Let's Go"
- [ ] "Good"
- why: "Go Sniff" opens free sniffing time, while "Let's Go" starts structured walking at your side.
