# World 5 · The Busy Park

## primer
### card 1
Remember: each Distractor fills the cup. Keep distance and take sniffing pauses between them, and if it overflows, leave the park.
- caption: Each Distractor fills the cup; sniffing empties it

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why does the Dog get overwhelmed if it can handle each thing on its own?
- [x] Arousal from each Distractor stacks up
- [ ] With so much movement it forgets its Cues
- [ ] It needs to run more to burn off energy
- why: Each Distractor raises its Arousal and takes minutes to come down; if another arrives before that, it stacks until the cup overflows.

### tq2
A bicycle just passed and the Dog handled it well. What now?
- [x] A sniffing pause, away from the path
- [ ] Keep walking, since it went well
- [ ] A short run to blow off steam
- why: After each Distractor, a sniffing pause lowers Arousal before the next one arrives.

### tq3
The Dog no longer takes treats and cannot hear you. What do you do?
- [x] Turn around and leave the park
- [ ] Bring out a higher-value treat and keep going
- [ ] Repeat the Cue louder and more often
- why: If the Dog can no longer eat or hear you, it is over its threshold, the point where it cannot listen: turn around and leave the park together.

## exercises
- tension: In the park: learn to keep the long leash Slack even when there is lots of movement.
- timing: In the park: use the Marker for calm as soon as your Dog relaxes after an encounter.
- attention: In the park: ask for a check-in before each path crossing.
