# World 4 · The Real Street

## primer
### card 1
Remember: stationary on the ground, "Leave It"; if it moves, "Let's Go". If neither fits, create more distance.
- caption: Stationary on the ground, "Leave It"; if it moves, "Let's Go"

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why do you not use "Leave It" with a scooter?
- [x] It approaches, and moving away together creates distance
- [ ] The Dog ignores it better if you stay still
- [ ] It only works at home, not outside
- why: Moving objects approach and raise Arousal with every second; moving away together with "Let's Go" creates distance, which lowers Arousal.

### tq2
You see bread on the sidewalk and the Dog has not seen it yet. What do you do?
- [x] Say "Leave It" once, before reaching it
- [ ] Repeat "Leave It" until you pass it
- [ ] Wait until the Dog sniffs it and then speak
- why: Stationary items on the ground call for "Leave It", said once and before the Dog reaches them.

### tq3
You say "Let's Go" and the Dog does not respond. What do you do?
- [x] Increase distance: cross the street or change routes
- [ ] Repeat "Let's Go" louder until it comes
- [ ] Switch to "Leave It" in case that works better
- why: If the Dog does not respond, do not demand more: gain distance, cross the sidewalk, change routes, or head home.
