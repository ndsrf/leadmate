# World 7 · Settling at the Table

## primer
### card 1
Remember: begging is never paid. Wait for {name} to lie down on the mat by choice, then say "good" and drop a treat.
- caption: The more calm, the more the treat is worth

### card 2
In the game, watch how {name} is and tap what helps: Ignore if the Dog begs at the table, Wait if it watches the waiter, and Marker when it sits to look at you or lies down calm. Feeding from the table is never the answer.

## checkpoint
### tq1
{name} sits by the table and stares at you. What do you do?
- [x] Do not look and do not hand over anything from the table; wait
- [ ] Hand over a little bite to calm the Dog down
- [ ] Talk to the Dog so it stops begging
- why: If begging works, the Dog will do more of it. Waiting without responding makes begging useless, and you pay the calm as soon as it arrives.

### tq2
When does the treat land?
- [ ] When {name} lies down calm on the mat and the treat lands quietly between the paws
- [ ] When {name} sits whining by the table and gets a bite
- why: The treat pays off when it arrives with the Dog lying down. If it arrives with the begging, it teaches begging.

### tq3
{name} had been on the mat for a while and now gets up and pants. What do you do?
- [x] Shorten the visit and leave before it gets worse
- [ ] Make the Dog lie down again
- [ ] Hand over table food to calm the Dog down
- why: Getting up, panting or whining are signs of tiredness. Leaving in time, while {name} is still calm, leaves a good memory for next time.

## exercises
- timing: On a terrace: say "good" and drop the treat only when {name} lies down by choice.
- tension: On a terrace: if {name} begs, do not respond and wait without touching the leash.
