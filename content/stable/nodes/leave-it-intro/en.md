# World 4 · Leave It

## primer
### card 1
Remember: say "Leave It" once, as soon as the Dog spots the food, and reward from your hand when it turns toward you.
- caption: The Dog turns toward you before reaching it: "good" and a reward

### card 2
In the game, tap Leave It, the open hand, as soon as the Dog looks at the food.

## checkpoint
### tq1
What does the Dog learn if you run to snatch food away?
- [x] That it must eat faster
- [ ] That food on the ground does not belong to it
- [ ] That it must wait until you arrive
- why: If you rush to take it away, the Dog learns to eat quickly before you get there.

### tq2
When do you say "Leave It" on a walk?
- [x] Once, as soon as the Dog spots the food
- [ ] When its nose is already touching it
- [ ] Multiple times, until it walks away
- why: Say it only once, as soon as the Dog sees the food and before reaching it: repeating it late does not help it succeed.

### tq3
You say "Leave It" and the Dog turns toward you. What do you do?
- [x] Give a treat from my hand
- [ ] Let it take the food from the ground
- [ ] Repeat "Leave It" to reinforce it
- why: Turning toward you earns "good" and a treat from your hand; the reward never comes from the ground.

## exercises
- attention: At home: place a treat on the floor, say "leave it", and reward when the Dog looks at you.
- timing: At home: reward right when your Dog turns its muzzle away from the treat.
