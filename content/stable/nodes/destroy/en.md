# At Home · Destroying Things

## primer
### card 1
Remember: what is out of reach cannot be destroyed. Put away forbidden items, provide chew toys, and trade without scolding.
- caption: Forbidden items, put away; approved items, within reach

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why does the Dog destroy things?
- [x] Chewing is a natural need and the Dog is bored
- [ ] To spite you
- [ ] The Dog knows it is wrong and does it anyway
- why: Chewing is a canine need: the Dog does not destroy out of malice or spite.

### tq2
You see the Dog chewing a shoe. What do you do?
- [x] Trade the shoe for an approved chew
- [ ] Scold it so it lets go
- [ ] Wait for it to get tired
- why: Trading calmly for an approved chew teaches what it can chew; scolding does not.

### tq3
You return home to find something destroyed. What do you do?
- [x] Clean up and put away what was within reach
- [ ] Scold the Dog so it understands
- [ ] Show the destruction up close
- why: The Dog does not connect a scolding with what happened hours ago; what works is removing forbidden items from its reach.

## exercises
- timing: At home: look around at the Dog's eye level and put away anything it could chew.
- tension: At home: leave two approved chews around and trade forbidden items for one of them.
