# At Home · Barking at Home

## primer
### card 1
Remember: remove the cause of barking, wait without adding noise, and reward silence as soon as it arrives.
- caption: Without a cause, barking decreases; with silence, comes reward

## checkpoint
### tq1
What do you do if your Dog barks at the window?
- [x] Remove the cause: close the curtain
- [ ] Yell "quiet" louder
- [ ] Give a treat so it stops
- why: What reduces barking is removing the cause, not adding noise or rewarding the bark.

### tq2
When do you say "good"?
- [x] As soon as there is a silent pause
- [ ] While it keeps barking
- [ ] Once an hour has passed
- why: What gets rewarded gets repeated: use the Marker and reward silence, even if it is brief.

### tq3
What if the Dog barks to demand attention?
- [x] I do not look at it or speak to it until it is quiet
- [ ] I talk to it to calm it down
- [ ] I stare at it until it stops
- why: Looking at or speaking to the Dog rewards the bark; when it is quiet or lies down, give it attention.

## exercises
- timing: At home: when the Dog barks, remove the cause or wait without speaking and reward the first pause.
- tension: At home: note how many seconds of silence the Dog achieves before the reward.
