# World 1 · Calm First

## primer
### card 1
Remember: calm first. While the Dog barks or runs, ask for nothing; when it sits and looks at you, give your Cue.
- caption: Calm first; then, the Cue

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why does the Dog not learn while barking continuously?
- [x] It is so alert that your words are just noise
- [ ] Because it does not want to listen to you
- [ ] Because your Cue is not clear enough
- why: A highly aroused Dog only pays attention to what set it off: in that state, your words are just noise.

### tq2
The Dog barks continuously at a visitor. What do you do?
- [x] Wait without moving or speaking
- [ ] Repeat "quiet" louder
- [ ] Give a treat so it stops
- why: Do not add energy: no Cues, no yelling, no treats. Wait motionlessly for it to calm down.

### tq3
When do you ask for something with your Cue?
- [x] When it sits and looks at you
- [ ] While barking, to interrupt it
- [ ] While running, to slow it down
- why: As soon as it sits or lies down and looks at you, it can hear you: then, give your Cue.

## exercises
- timing: At home: if the Dog barks nonstop, wait without speaking for it to calm down before asking for anything.
- tension: At home: use the Marker with a gentle "good" each time it lies down calmly.
