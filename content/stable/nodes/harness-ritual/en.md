# World 2 · The Harness Ritual

## primer
### card 1
Remember: the harness only approaches when the Dog is still. If it jumps, bring the harness back to your chest and wait.
- caption: If it jumps, the harness moves away; if it is still, it approaches

### card 2
In the game, observe its state at each step and tap the action that helps: wait, call, use the Marker, or put on the harness.

## checkpoint
### tq1
What does the Dog learn if you put on the harness while it is jumping?
- [x] That jumping makes the walk happen sooner
- [ ] That it can calm down now because it is going outside
- [ ] That it must wait until you finish
- why: If the harness arrives while jumping, jumping earns the walk; that is why the harness only approaches when the Dog is still.

### tq2
The Dog stops jumping and starts sniffing around. What do you do?
- [x] Say its name once, in a soft voice
- [ ] Put the harness on while it is distracted
- [ ] Keep waiting until it sits down on its own
- why: If distracted, say its name once in a soft voice so it looks back at you; the harness arrives when it is still and attentive.

### tq3
You bring the harness close and the Dog jumps again. What do you do?
- [x] Bring it back to my chest and wait motionlessly
- [ ] Put it on quickly since it is close
- [ ] Repeat "stay" until it stops
- why: If it jumps, the harness returns to your chest and you wait without moving: while jumping, nothing happens.

## exercises
- poste: At home: before going out, wait motionlessly until your Dog sits, and only then put on the harness.
- tension: At home: if it gets overexcited, put the harness down and count to five before trying again.
