# World 6 · Not That One

## primer
### card 1
Remember: food is never for sniffing. Say "Leave It" before {name} reaches it, and when {name} looks at you instead, mark the look and press the Jackpot.
- caption: Choosing you over the food earns the star

## checkpoint
### tq1
There is a chip by the bin ahead and {name} stares at it. What do you do?
- [x] Say "Leave It" before {name} gets there
- [ ] Say "Go Sniff" so {name} can check it
- [ ] Tug hard to pull {name} past
- why: Food is not a smell to read. "Leave It" before it is reached tells {name} what to do instead.

### tq2
When is the Jackpot worth pressing?
- [x] When {name} looks at you with food or a strong smell near
- [ ] On every look, to be safe
- [ ] On an empty path, when {name} is calm
- why: A Jackpot is for hard moments. On an easy look it is wasted and runs out.

### tq3
{name} turns away from the food and looks at you. What do you give?
- [x] Mark the look and press the Jackpot
- [ ] Nothing, since {name} should not have looked at the food
- [ ] A "Go Sniff" at the food, as a reward
- why: Choosing you over food was hard. A big reward shows it paid off, and food stays off the menu.

## exercises
- tension: Outside: when you see food or rubbish ahead, say "Leave It" and keep the leash Slack until you have passed.
- attention: Outside: reward each look away from food with something better than usual.
