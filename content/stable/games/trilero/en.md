# Shell game

## intro
Three cups upside down with kibble under one. Your dog learns that the treat comes from your hand, not from the cup, and practises self-control: looking without lunging, waiting, and choosing well.

## materials
- Three opaque plastic cups, big and stable
- A handful of kibble or tiny treats

## steps
1. Put the three cups upside down on the floor, spaced apart, and hide a piece of kibble under one.
2. If your dog tries to lift a cup with its nose, cover it with your hand and wait for your dog to back off.
3. As soon as it stops pushing and looks at you, say "good" and give a piece of kibble from your other hand.
4. Repeat until your dog looks at the cups without pushing them. The kibble under the cup is only uncovered when you decide.
5. When your dog is calm, say "now" and let it discover the kibble under the cup as an extra gift.

## tips
- Start with a single cup; add the second and third when your dog does it calmly.
- If it gets frustrated, shorten the session and finish with something it already knows.
- Always reward from your hand, never from the cup: that way your dog learns to ask you for things.

## safety
Supervise your dog while it plays with the cups. If it starts chewing them, take them away: swallowed plastic is dangerous. Use cups bigger than your dog's mouth and stable, with no broken edges, and put them away when done.
