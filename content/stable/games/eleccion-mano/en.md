# Pick a hand

## intro
A two-minute game that teaches your dog that its choices pay off. You close two fists and your dog picks one by touching it with a paw. It helps calm excitement and builds the habit of thinking before acting.

## materials
- A handful of kibble or tiny treats
- A quiet spot with no distractions

## steps
1. Sit facing your dog with a treat in each hand and close both fists at chest height.
2. Wait calmly. Let your dog sniff, lick or nudge the fists; do not open either one yet.
3. As soon as a paw touches a fist, say "good" and open that hand so your dog can eat the treat.
4. A nose touch instead of a paw counts at the start: what matters is that your dog picks one hand.
5. Repeat about ten times and finish with one last treat as a gift. No rush: it is normal for it to take a few seconds at first.

## tips
- If your dog gets worked up or barks, move the fists a little further away and wait for a breath before you go on.
- Once it is easy, offer one empty hand and one with a treat, and let your dog find out which one pays.
- Use kibble from the daily ration so you do not add extra food.
