# Snuffle blanket

## intro
A snuffle mat hides kibble among its strips and your dog has to dig its nose in to find it. It is the most complete sniffing game for rainy days: it calms and tires the mind.

## materials
- A snuffle mat (fabric strips)
- A handful of kibble from the daily ration

## steps
1. Put the snuffle mat on the floor, on a surface that does not slide.
2. Hide a few pieces of kibble among the strips, leaving some in view so your dog understands the game.
3. Let your dog sniff. Say "find it" calmly and do not push: every find is its reward.
4. Once it has the hang of it, hide the kibble deeper and use more pieces.
5. When done, pick the mat up before your dog starts chewing the strips.

## tips
- It is normal for your dog to pull at the strips or flip the mat over at first; let it try.
- To make it harder, tie the strips in knots or add more layers.
- Use the mat only when you are nearby and can see your dog.

## safety
Always supervise your dog while it uses the mat. If it starts chewing or pulling at the strips, take it away: swallowed fabric can block the gut. Check that the mat has no loose threads or torn strips, and store it out of reach when the game ends.
