# How to Play: Walking

## intro
From now on you walk along the street. Here are the walk controls on the actual screen.

## Control 1
### The Joystick
Drag the joystick to walk. The further you move it from the center, the faster you move. Release it to stop.
- alt: Screenshot of the walk with the joystick on the bottom left and a directional arrow
- caption: Walking with the joystick

## Control 2
### The Leash
The leash hangs green when Slack, turns straight and yellow when Taut, and turns red and thicker if it becomes a Rigid Pull.
- alt: Screenshot of the walk with the straight, Taut leash between Handler and Dog, highlighted
- caption: Taut leash: straight and yellow

## Control 3
### Poste
If you release the joystick when the leash goes Taut, you perform Poste: a light ring appears at your feet. Wait until the Dog returns and the leash goes Slack.
- alt: Screenshot of the walk with the joystick released and the Poste ring at the Handler's feet, highlighted
- caption: Performing Poste

## Control 4
### Go Sniff
When your Dog has walked nicely, the Go Sniff button appears for a short time with a depleting ring. Tap it before it expires: sniffing is their reward.
- alt: Screenshot of the walk with the Go Sniff button highlighted
- caption: Allow sniffing

## tip
Test the controls without rushing: the first walk is for learning, not scoring.
