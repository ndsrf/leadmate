# How to Play: Choose the Action

## intro
In home Minigames, your Dog appears in each step in a different state. Your job is to read them and tap the action that helps. Here is how it looks on the actual screen.

## Control 1
### Read your Dog
Above the buttons you see your Dog and a phrase describing what they are doing, such as "Asks to be picked up" or "Up on the sofa". Pay attention to their body, ears, and tail.
- alt: Screenshot of the living room minigame: the Dog asks to be picked up and the descriptive phrase is highlighted
- caption: Read your Dog

## Control 2
### Tap the action
Below are four buttons, such as Ignore, Send to bed, Marker, or Cuddle. Tap the one that helps in that moment; a single tap is enough. If you make a mistake, you will see their reaction and a hint will point out what to watch.
- alt: Screenshot of the living room minigame with the four action buttons and the Ignore button highlighted
- caption: Tap the action that helps

## tip
There is no rush: you have several seconds on each step. Look first, tap second.
