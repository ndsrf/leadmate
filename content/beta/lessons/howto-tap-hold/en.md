# How to Play: Tap and Hold

## intro
In home Minigames, you use two gestures: tap, which you already know, and hold. Here is each one on the actual screen.

## Control 1
### Tap
Just like in the living room: observe your Dog and tap the action that helps. Here they are Wait, Cue, Marker, or the step-specific action.

- caption: Tap the action that helps

## Control 2
### Hold
In Poste practice, hold the button down to remain stationary while the Dog pulls. Release only once they have calmed and the leash goes Slack.
- alt: Screenshot of Poste practice with the hold button highlighted
- caption: Hold to perform Poste

## tip
Look at the Dog, not the button: their ears and tail tell you when to release.
