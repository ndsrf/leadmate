# Sound Desensitization

## heading
Household sounds without fear

## hook
You turn on the vacuum cleaner, the doorbell rings, or thunder rumbles in the distance, and {name} startles, bolts under the table, or barks in alarm. If not addressed early, these small frights can develop into severe sound phobias in adulthood.

## why
During their first months of life, puppies navigate a critical window of socialization and habituation. Any auditory Distractor experienced in association with calm and pleasant sensations becomes part of everyday normalcy. If volume exceeds their tolerance threshold and panic sets in, sensitization occurs—fear deepens with each occurrence. Systematic desensitization means introducing the sound at a minimal, subthreshold volume while the puppy enjoys something delicious or rests, increasing intensity only when the prior level produces complete indifference.

## steps
1. Select a common sound that tends to startle them: the doorbell, vacuum, fireworks, or a thunderstorm, prepared on a phone or speaker.
2. Play the sound at the lowest possible volume, barely audible, in another room or at a generous distance while {name} rests or enjoys a lick mat.
3. Observe their body language: if they continue eating or resting without pricking ears or stiffening their body, the volume is appropriate.
4. Keep sessions very short, just two to three minutes, and turn off the sound before they lose interest in their food.
5. Increase the volume very gradually over subsequent days. If at any point they tense up or stop eating, immediately lower the volume to the previous level.

## example
### {name} and the doorbell chime
Every time the doorbell rings, {name} startles in alarm. You prepare a recording of the doorbell chime on your phone with the volume near minimum. You give {name} a chew or stuffed toy on their bed. While chewing calmly, you play the chime once at low volume. {name} flicks an ear but keeps chewing without standing up. You speak a soft "good" Marker. You repeat twice and put the phone away. The following day, you can raise the volume by a single notch.

## compare
### do
Work at minimal, subthreshold volume, pairing the sound with chewing or peaceful rest.
### dont
Expose them to loud noises abruptly expecting them to "get used to it." Flooding causes chronic fear and panic.

## tip
Try it today: play a thunderstorm or vacuum track on your phone at the lowest volume for two minutes while your puppy enjoys dinner.

## takeaways
- Always work below the puppy's alert threshold.
- Pair the auditory Distractor with high-value food or calming chewing.
- If fear appears or eating stops, lower volume and increase distance.
- A few minutes daily during puppyhood prevent adult phobias.
