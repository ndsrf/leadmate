# Sniffing on Your Terms

## heading
Some smells are for waiting, and some are not for you to give

## hook
Some smells are strong enough that {name} simply stops: the legs lock, the nose goes down and no tug will move it. A tug is a bad idea anyway, because the leash goes Taut and the smell is gone before it was read. Other times it is the opposite: there is a chip on the pavement or a bin that smells of dinner, and {name} wants it badly. Both moments are about the same thing: who decides what gets sniffed, and when.

## why
When {name} Plants, it is reading something important, and waiting with a Slack leash costs you a few seconds. Dragging a Plant away teaches {name} that pulling is how smells get read, and it makes the next one stronger. A dog may also lift a leg at a post to leave its own mark. This is not the Marker, which is your "good" and the Marker button; it is {name} writing a message. Let it finish too. Food is different. Food is not a smell to read, it is a prize that would be eaten, and "Go Sniff" must never lead there. Teach "Leave It" and pay with something better. For a hard moment like this, use a high-value reward: a Jackpot, much better than the usual treat. Keep it for moments where choosing you over the smell or the food was difficult.

## steps
1. When {name} Plants at a strong smell or stops at a post to leave a mark, stop too, keep the leash Slack in a U and wait until the head comes up.
2. Do not call, tug or say "Let's Go" until {name} has finished; then say "Let's Go" and walk on together.
3. When you see food or rubbish ahead, say "Leave It" before {name} reaches it, and keep the leash Slack. Never give "Go Sniff" at food.
4. When {name} looks at you instead of the food, mark the look and give a Jackpot: a handful of treats or something extra special, not the usual single piece.

## example
### {name} by the bin outside the café
{name} slows beside the lamp post at the café and plants. You stop, keep the leash hanging in a U and wait. After about five seconds {name} lifts the head and you say "Let's Go". Two steps later there is a chip beside the bin. {name} stares at it. You say "Leave It" and wait. {name} glances up at you, you mark the look and give a Jackpot of three small treats, one after another. {name} walks on with the head up.

## compare
### do
Wait out a Plant or a marking post with a Slack leash, say "Leave It" before food and save the Jackpot for the hard moment when {name} chooses you.
### dont
Drag {name} away from a Plant, give "Go Sniff" at food, or spend the Jackpot on an easy moment when nothing was hard.

## tip
Try it today: on your next walk, wait out one strong smell without pulling. If you meet food or rubbish, say "Leave It" and reward the look with something special.

## takeaways
- A Plant is {name} reading something important: wait with a Slack leash.
- Dragging a Plant or a marking post away teaches pulling.
- Never give "Go Sniff" at food; use "Leave It" and pay with something better.
- A Jackpot is for hard moments, so choose them.
