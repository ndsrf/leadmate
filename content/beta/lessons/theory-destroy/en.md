# Destroying Things

## heading
You return home to find a shredded shoe

## hook
You open the front door and there it is: a chewed shoe, a gutted cushion, or door molding covered in teeth marks. Frustration surges, and {name} looks at you as if "knowing" what happened. But that is not guilt: they are simply reacting to your body language and tone. This problem can be resolved, and scolding is not the way.

## why
Chewing is an essential canine need: dogs explore with their mouths, self-soothe, pass the time, and relieve dental discomfort. Destruction of household items happens when boredom strikes, pent-up energy overflows, or appropriate chewing outlets have not been taught. Scolding after the fact achieves nothing: a Dog cannot connect your anger with something done hours ago. What works is dog-proofing your home and keeping safe chews accessible. If destruction occurs only while you are away, accompanied by barking or whining, it could be separation anxiety: consult a professional.

## steps
1. Pick up shoes, remote controls, laundry, and cords: what is out of reach cannot be destroyed. Close doors to rooms not in use.
2. Distribute safe chews throughout the home: appropriately sized durable toys, rubber chewers, and food-stuffed toys.
3. If you catch them chewing an off-limits item, cheerfully offer a chew toy in trade, and say "good" the moment they take it.
4. Meet mental and physical needs before leaving: a walk with generous sniffing time and interactive play. A satisfied Dog settles down to sleep.
5. Maintain predictable routines: consistent meal and walk times alongside daily dedicated attention reduce boredom and restlessness.

## example
### {name} and the slippers
Every morning after returning from grocery shopping, you find slippers chewed at the heels. This week, you store them inside the closet and leave a food-stuffed toy in the living room. One afternoon, {name} wanders toward the shoe rack; you offer the chew toy in trade, and they happily begin gnawing on it. You say "good" the instant they engage with it. Upon returning home, the slippers are untouched and the toy is emptied clean.

## compare
### do
Leave appropriate chews within reach and calmly trade off-limits items for them.
### dont
Scold upon discovering destruction after the fact: they do not understand why, which can cause them to fear you.

## tip
Today: walk through your home at {name}'s eye level and stow away anything chewable. Place two safe chews in their resting spot.

## takeaways
- Chewing is a biological need: provide appropriate outlets.
- What is out of reach cannot be destroyed.
- Trade off-limits items for safe chews.
- Scolding after the fact achieves nothing.
