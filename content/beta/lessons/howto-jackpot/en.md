# How to Play: the Jackpot Button

## heading
A small button for the hard moments

## hook
From this Stage on, a small star button appears next to the Marker button. It is the Jackpot. It works like the Marker but pays far more, and you only get a few of them per Stage, so it matters when you press it.

## why
The Marker (see Marking and Rewarding) tells {name} "that was right". A Jackpot says the same with a much bigger reward, and it is worth it when {name} chose you in a hard moment: there is food beside the path, a strong smell is close, or something is passing. A Jackpot on an easy look, on an empty path, is wasted, and you will see a message saying so. The badge on the button shows how many Jackpots are left. Press it instead of the Marker, never as well, and only after {name} looks at you.

## steps
1. Find the star button next to the Marker button. The number on it is your Jackpots left.
2. Walk as usual and watch for a hard moment: food or a strong smell near {name}.
3. When {name} looks at you at that moment, press the star button instead of the Marker, right as {name} looks.
4. On an easy look, press the Marker as usual and keep your Jackpots for later.

## example
### {name} near the bin
You are in the park and there is food beside the bin. {name} stares at it and you give "Leave It". {name} turns and looks at you. This is a hard moment, so you press the star button. A message says it was a smart Jackpot. Later, on an empty path, {name} looks at you for a second. This is an easy moment, so you press the Marker and keep the star.

## compare
### do
Press the star button when {name} looks at you with food or a strong smell close by.
### dont
Press the star button on every look; the Jackpots run out and the rest of the Stage gets only the Marker.

## tip
Try it today: in your next Stage, count your Jackpots and use each one only when {name} turns away from food or a strong smell to look at you.

## takeaways
- The star button is the Jackpot, a bigger reward than the Marker.
- You get only a few per Stage; the badge shows how many are left.
- Press it instead of the Marker, on a look in a hard moment.
- On an easy look, use the Marker and keep the Jackpot.
