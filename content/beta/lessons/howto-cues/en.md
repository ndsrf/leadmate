# How to Play: Cues

## intro
You now have Cue buttons. Here is where they are and when to use each one.

## Control 1
### Leave It
On streets with food on the ground, you have the Leave It button showing an open hand. Press it when your Dog looks at the food, before reaching it.

- caption: The Leave It button

## Control 2
### Let's Go
With two Cues, the green speech bubble says Let's Go: it prompts them to follow you and move away from fast-moving objects. Use it for scooters and bicycles.
- alt: Screenshot of the walk with the Let's Go button and an approaching scooter
- caption: The Let's Go button

## tip
One timely Cue is worth more than two late ones.
