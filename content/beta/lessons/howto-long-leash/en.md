# How to Play: The Long Leash

## intro
In the park, the leash is 3 meters long. It looks just like the short leash, but your Dog can reach much further.

## Control 1
### More Leash
With 3 meters, your Dog can move away and sniff while the leash continues hanging green and Slack. It only goes Taut when reaching the end.
- alt: Screenshot of the park with the Dog far from the Handler and the long leash hanging Slack, highlighted
- caption: Far away, but with a Slack leash

## Control 2
### Braking in Time
When the leash becomes straight and yellow, the prompt to brake appears. Release the joystick and use Poste: with 3 meters, the Dog reaches the end with momentum.
- alt: Screenshot of the park with the long leash Taut and the prompt to brake above, highlighted
- caption: Braking when the leash goes Taut

## tip
In the park, look even further ahead: joggers approach quickly.
