# Settling at the Table

## heading
You want to finish your coffee in peace

## hook
You sit down with your coffee and {name} stares at the table. {name} sits, looks at you and rests their snout on your knee. You hand over a little bite to stop the begging and, as soon as it is eaten, the begging starts again, a bit stronger each time. You cannot enjoy your coffee and {name} cannot rest: on guard, waiting for the next crumb.

## why
Begging at the table is learned. If a fixed stare or a snout on your knee gets food, {name} learns that insisting pays, and will do it more. If what pays is lying down on the mat, that is what gets learned. So begging is never paid: not with food from the table and not with attention. Calm is paid, with small treats that arrive quietly, and a chew gives the Dog something good to do meanwhile.

## steps
1. Spread the mat beside your chair before ordering anything and give {name} something to chew, so there is something good to do.
2. If {name} sits and stares or begs, do not look, do not talk and do not hand over anything from the table. Wait without doing anything.
3. As soon as {name} lies down on the mat by choice, say a soft "good" Marker and drop a small treat between the front paws, without getting up.
4. Read the calm scale: alert when sitting and watching you, calm when lying with the head resting, and fully calm when lying on one side.
5. If {name} does not settle, do not push: shorten the visit, move to a quieter table, or get up and leave. Missing the coffee is not a failure.

## example
### {name} and the mat by the table
You sit in the corner of the terrace and {name} lies on the mat with a chew. A waiter sets a plate on the next table and {name} sits up to watch. You do not look and you hand over nothing. A few seconds later {name} rests their head on the mat and lies down. You say your soft "good" Marker and drop a treat between the paws. At first you repeat it every few seconds; later, further apart. The first time, settling takes a while. The next time, a bit less.

## compare
### do
Drop a small treat between {name}'s paws when they lie down by choice, without leaving your chair and without talking much.
### dont
Hand over a bite from the table when {name} gets up and puts a snout to your hand. It feels kind, but it teaches that begging works.

## tip
Try it today: at home, sit at the table with a coffee and the mat beside your chair. Pay a small treat every time {name} lies down without begging.

## takeaways
- Never pay begging: no table food and no attention.
- Pay calm: a small treat when the Dog lies down by choice.
- The mat and a chew give the Dog something good to do.
- If the Dog does not settle, shorten the visit or leave.
