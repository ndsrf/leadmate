# Let Them Sniff

## target
On a walk, give "Go Sniff" at least three times, each right after {name} looks at you. Check back on a later day to see how it went.

## success
{name} looks at you before each sniff, and the leash stays Slack while {name} reads the smell.

## problems
### pulls-to-smells
- label: {name} pulls toward smells
- reassure: Many dogs rush at a smell that has always been out of reach. It eases as sniffing becomes part of the walk.
- cause: Smells have been won by pulling, so pulling still pays.
- c1: Stop and wait with a Slack leash until the leash hangs, then wait for a look.
- c2: Mark the look and give "Go Sniff" so the smell comes after the glance.
- easier: One smell on a quiet path, with a long wait.
- alt: A person waits with a Slack leash while the Dog stands near a bush
- caption: Wait for the look, then the smell

### nolook
- label: {name} does not look at me
- reassure: Glances take time to build. A quiet start is normal.
- cause: The smell is very strong, or the wait is too short.
- c1: Choose a weaker smell and wait a little longer.
- c2: Mark the first glance right away, even a tiny one.
- easier: A faint smell in a calm corner of the park.
- alt: A Dog glances up at its person near a lamp post
- caption: Reward every glance

### tight
- label: The leash goes Taut at smells
- reassure: Smells change where {name} wants to go, and the leash shows it first.
- cause: You moved on before {name} finished, or stood too far from the smell.
- c1: Step toward the smell so the leash hangs in a U again.
- c2: Say "Let's Go" only when {name} lifts the head.
- easier: Let {name} sniff close to your feet.
- alt: A person steps toward a bush so the leash hangs in a U
- caption: Keep the leash Slack while {name} sniffs

## general
- reassure: Letting your Dog sniff is a skill that grows over many walks.
- cause: New routines take a while to settle in for you and {name}.
- c1: Repeat the exercise on three different days.
- c2: Review the Let Them Sniff lesson.
- easier: Five minutes of sniffing in a quiet park.
- alt: A calm morning park path with a person and a Dog
- caption: Short, calm practice
