# A Short First Visit

## target
Visit a terrace for about 10 minutes with {name}: arrive after a sniffing walk, pick a corner by the wall and spread the mat. Before sitting down, ask the venue whether dogs are welcome.

## success
You sit down, {name} lies on the mat at some point, and you leave calmly before the Dog gets tired.

## problems
### pulls
- label: Pulls on the leash when arriving
- reassure: This is normal: a terrace full of smells and people excites many dogs, especially the first time.
- cause: The Dog probably arrives with energy still unspent or in a hurry along the way, and the leash goes tight.
- c1: Before the visit, take a longer sniffing walk and stop each time the leash goes tight.
- c2: If the excitement does not drop, stay outside at a distance for a while or leave the visit for another day.
- easier: Walk to the door on a slack leash and back, 3 times.
- alt: The Handler stops with a tight leash on the sidewalk and the Dog comes back to their side
- caption: Stopping so the leash goes slack

### food
- label: Lunges for food on the ground
- reassure: Something always drops near the tables. It is normal for a Dog with a good nose.
- cause: Food on the ground is the most interesting thing around, and the Dog does not yet know that looking at you pays more.
- c1: Say "Leave It" once, as soon as the Dog sees it and before it arrives, and reward from your hand when it turns toward you.
- c2: If there is a lot of food, pick another table or shorten the visit: more distance always helps.
- easier: Walk past a treat on the ground with "Leave It", 3 times.
- alt: The Dog turns toward the Handler before reaching a piece of food on the ground
- caption: Looking at you before arriving earns a reward

### nomat
- label: Will not lie down on the mat
- reassure: It is common for the first visit to take time to settle; ten minutes already counts.
- cause: Perhaps the mat does not smell of home yet, or there is too much movement near the table.
- c1: Use the same mat at home for a few days, with something to chew, so it feels familiar.
- c2: Move to a table with less foot traffic or shorten the visit: leaving in time also counts.
- easier: Stay on the mat for one minute at home, 3 times.
- alt: The Dog chews a toy on the mat in the living room
- caption: The mat smells of home

## general
- reassure: A short first visit that ends well is worth more than a long one that ends badly.
- cause: The most common mistake is staying too long, especially when everything is going well.
- c1: Plan ten minutes and leave at ten, even if it feels like you could stay longer.
- c2: Review the "Getting Ready for the Café" lesson.
- easier: Do a five-minute visit.
- alt: The Handler and the Dog walk away from the terrace on a slack leash
- caption: Leaving in time leaves a good memory
