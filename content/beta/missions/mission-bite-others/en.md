# Protecting Others

## target
For three days, avoid the situations with people that are hardest for {name}; when unavoidable, place a closed door or gate between them and {name}, and log any warning signals you observe. To practice calm around hands, play the first exercise of World 1.

## success
No one approached {name} without permission, and you logged warning signals without bites.

## problems
### approach
- label: Someone approached anyway
- reassure: This happens to many Handlers: strangers often cannot read a Dog's warning signals.
- cause: The other person likely did not realize they needed to maintain distance.
- c1: Use Handler Shielding between {name} and the person, increase distance, and say calmly: "Please don't greet right now."
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Walk during the quietest hours for one day.
- alt: The Handler positions between the Dog and a stranger with a calming gesture
- caption: Handler Shielding

### growl
- label: Growled or lunged
- reassure: The Dog gave a warning, which is the best communication before a bite: respect it.
- cause: The distance was likely too close for what {name} can tolerate today.
- c1: Increase distance immediately without yanking the leash or scolding, and retreat from the situation.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Avoid unfamiliar people for an entire day and log warning signals at home.
- alt: The Handler and Dog walk slowly away from another person
- caption: More distance

### cantmanage
- label: Could not avoid the situation
- reassure: You cannot always control everything; reducing encounters is enough.
- cause: Sometimes the situation depends on others, such as a shared building hallway or doorway.
- c1: Change the time or route of the walk, or use an alternate exit, until you find a path with few people.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: A single walk at a quiet time.
- alt: An empty street early in the morning
- caption: Fewer people, less risk

## general
- reassure: Protecting others and {name} takes dedication. Every incident-free day counts.
- cause: Trying to tackle everything at once is common.
- c1: Avoid the most difficult triggers first and record the warning signs you observe.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Avoid a single difficult situation for one day.
- alt: A notebook with a short list and a pencil
- caption: Start by avoiding the hardest situations
