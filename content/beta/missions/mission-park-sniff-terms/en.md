# Sniffing on Your Terms

## target
On a walk, wait out two sniffing stops without pulling, and say "Leave It" once on food or rubbish. Check back on a later day to see how it went.

## success
You wait with a Slack leash while {name} finishes, and {name} turns from the food or rubbish when you say "Leave It".

## problems
### drags-plant
- label: I pull {name} away from smells
- reassure: It is a natural reflex when you are in a hurry or worried. Waiting feels long at first.
- cause: Waiting feels like a delay, so the leash goes Taut before {name} has finished.
- c1: Stop when {name} stops and let the leash hang in a U.
- c2: Count slowly to ten before you say "Let's Go".
- easier: Choose a quiet path and a smell {name} likes, with no hurry.
- alt: A person waits with a Slack leash while the Dog stands at a lamp post
- caption: Wait for the head to come up

### eats-food
- label: {name} eats food or rubbish
- reassure: Food on the ground is the best prize there is for a dog. Even a well-trained dog gets it sometimes.
- cause: The food was reached before you said "Leave It", or "Leave It" has not been practised enough.
- c1: Say "Leave It" as soon as you see food, before {name} gets there.
- c2: Practise "Leave It" at home first, and review the Leave It lesson.
- easier: Practise with a treat in a closed hand on a quiet street.
- alt: A person keeps the leash short beside food on the ground
- caption: Say it before the food is reached

### tight
- label: The leash goes Taut when we wait
- reassure: A stopped dog at a strong smell can feel like a tug. It is easier to avoid with practice.
- cause: You stopped too far from {name}, or turned to walk on early.
- c1: Step toward {name} so the leash hangs in a U again.
- c2: Stay still until the head comes up.
- easier: Wait right beside {name} on a short leash.
- alt: A person steps toward the Dog so the leash hangs in a U
- caption: Keep the leash Slack while you wait

## general
- reassure: Waiting and leaving things are skills that grow over many walks.
- cause: New routines take a while to settle in for you and {name}.
- c1: Repeat the exercise on three different days.
- c2: Review the Sniffing on Your Terms lesson.
- easier: Five minutes of waiting at a smell in a quiet park.
- alt: A calm park path with a person and a Dog
- caption: Short, calm practice
