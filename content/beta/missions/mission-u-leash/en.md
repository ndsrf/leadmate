# A U-Leash Walk

## target
Walk an entire street block with Slack in a U-shape, rewarding when the Dog returns to your side.

## success
The leash hangs in a relaxed U-shape for most of the block.

## problems
### lost
- label: The U-shape disappears right away
- reassure: Maintaining the U takes practice. Managing part of a block is real progress.
- cause: You are likely walking faster or slower than the Dog.
- c1: Match your walking pace to the Dog's during the first few minutes.
- c2: Slow down the moment the U-shape straightens, before tension sets in.
- easier: Half a block with Slack in a U-shape.
- alt: A Handler and Dog walking at the same pace with the leash hanging in a U-shape
- caption: Matching pace

### zigzag
- label: Drifts from side to side
- reassure: Zigzagging is how Dogs map the world through scent.
- cause: Abundant scents compete for attention, and the desired walking side is not yet clear.
- c1: Always deliver rewards on the same side.
- c2: Offer sniffing breaks as an environmental reward.
- easier: Returns to your side 3 times along one block.
- alt: A Dog receives a reward consistently on the same side
- caption: Reward on the same side

### treats
- label: Only returns for food
- reassure: Returning for food is an effective starting foundation.
- cause: The treat is visible before any behavior occurs.
- c1: Keep the treat tucked away and produce it only after pressing the Marker.
- c2: Alternate food rewards with opportunities to sniff.
- easier: Use the Marker 3 times as the Dog turns back to your side, with treats concealed.
- alt: A Handler retrieves a treat from a pocket after using the Marker
- caption: Mark first, then deliver the treat

## general
- reassure: A consistent U-leash is built walk by walk.
- cause: Expecting a continuous U-shape from the very first step is the common trap.
- c1: Count successful U-loops, not the pulls.
- c2: Conclude the walk immediately after a strong, relaxed stretch.
- easier: A short street segment with the leash hanging in a U-shape.
- alt: A leash hanging in a relaxed U-shape over a short distance
- caption: Count the U-shapes
