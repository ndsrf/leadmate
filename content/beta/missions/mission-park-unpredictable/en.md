# The Unpredictable Walk

## target
On a walk in an open space, change direction without warning at least five times and reward each Check-in. Check back on a later day to see how {name} responds.

## success
{name} looks for you on most turns, and the leash stays Slack through every change of direction.

## problems
### autopilot
- label: {name} stays on autopilot
- reassure: Many dogs follow the route they know. Changing it takes a few walks to pay off.
- cause: The turns were too predictable, or you named each one before making it.
- c1: Turn without speaking and let {name} notice the change on their own.
- c2: Mix the rhythm: a turn, a pause, then a few steps straight.
- easier: Three turns on a quiet path, rewarding each look.
- alt: A handler turns left on a park path while the Dog notices and looks back
- caption: Turn first, let your Dog find you

### nolook
- label: {name} does not look at me
- reassure: Glances take time to build. A quiet start is normal.
- cause: The surroundings are too interesting, or the reward comes too late.
- c1: Walk in a calmer place and reward the first glance right away.
- c2: Use "Go Sniff" after a few good turns so sniffing is part of the deal.
- easier: A few unexpected turns in a quiet corner of the park, 2 minutes.
- alt: A Dog glances up at the handler on a quiet grass path
- caption: Reward every glance

### tight
- label: The leash goes Taut on turns
- reassure: Turns change the leash shape, and that takes practice for both of you.
- cause: You turned too sharply while {name} was still ahead.
- c1: Slow down before the turn so the leash stays Slack.
- c2: Use "Let's Go" to set off again once the leash hangs in a U.
- easier: Wide, gentle turns in an open space.
- alt: A handler makes a wide turn with the leash hanging in a loose U
- caption: Wide turns keep the leash Slack

## general
- reassure: The Unpredictable Walk is a skill that grows over many walks.
- cause: New routines take a while to settle in for you and {name}.
- c1: Repeat a short Unpredictable Walk on three different days.
- c2: Review the Unpredictable Walks lesson.
- easier: Five minutes of easy turns in a quiet park.
- alt: A calm morning park path with a handler and a Dog
- caption: Short, calm practice
