# Preventing Bites

## target
For three days, log each time {name} growls or bites, and manage one trigger scenario; for example, give chews in a separate room behind a closed door. To practice calm near hands, play the first exercise in World 1.

## success
You logged every warning signal, managed the chosen situation, and avoided any bites.

## problems
### growls
- label: Keeps growling
- reassure: A growl is communication, not failure: it signals discomfort before escalating to a bite.
- cause: The trigger is likely still present: high-value food, resting space, or approaching at that moment.
- c1: Step back and give distance as soon as a growl occurs, without speaking or staring.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Manage a single scenario for one day and record if growling occurs.
- alt: The Handler calmly steps back, giving space to the Dog
- caption: Giving space

### bit
- label: Bit again
- reassure: Setbacks happen, and it does not mean failure. Safety for everyone is the immediate priority.
- cause: The Dog encountered an unmanaged situation, or subtle warning signals were missed.
- c1: Clean any wound and seek medical care if punctured. Separate {name} behind a door gently, without confrontation.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Manage the biting trigger for an entire day and reach out to a behavior professional.
- alt: A closed door safely separates the Dog from the Handler
- caption: Separate first

### cantavoid
- label: Cannot avoid the situation
- reassure: You cannot change everything at once; start with what is manageable.
- cause: The situation often involves daily habits, like mealtimes or furniture access, where changing routines is hard.
- c1: Pick a single scenario to modify: meals served in a quiet room behind a closed door, or a mat on the floor instead of the sofa.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Change only mealtime location for one day.
- alt: A closed door with the Dog eating calmly on the other side
- caption: Change one thing at a time

## general
- reassure: Changing household habits takes time, and second-guessing is normal. Every bite-free day is progress.
- cause: Trying to overhaul too many routines at once is common.
- c1: Start with a single scenario and log what happens immediately before any warning signal.
- c2: If {name} bites hard or frequently, consult a canine behavior professional promptly. This app does not replace professional care.
- easier: Log warning signals for just one day.
- alt: A notebook with a concise list and a pencil
- caption: One step at a time
