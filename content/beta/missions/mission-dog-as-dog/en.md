# A Day as a Dog

## target
Spend a full day without picking the Dog up, without allowing it on your bed, and without kissing its face. Greet only when calm is present.

## success
By the end of the day, demanding to be held decreases and resting in its own bed increases.

## problems
### cries
- label: Whines to be picked up
- reassure: This is very common: if whining previously resulted in being held, it has a reinforced history.
- cause: The Dog learned that whining brings physical elevation, prompting an extinction burst.
- c1: Do not pick the Dog up while it whines. Wait without eye contact: yielding now teaches longer whining.
- c2: Once silent with all four paws grounded, kneel down and stroke its chest gently at Dog level.
- easier: Ensure quiet with four paws on the floor before petting, 3 times.
- alt: A Handler crouches down to stroke the chest of the Dog, which has all four paws on the ground
- caption: Affection at ground level

### family
- label: Not everyone at home follows the rules
- reassure: Common across households: different family members handle the Dog inconsistently.
- cause: Someone continues picking up or inviting onto furniture, confusing the Dog.
- c1: Coordinate with household members and agree on three rules: paws on floor, sleep in own bed, and no face kissing.
- c2: Clarify the reasoning: it is not showing less love; it is respecting the Dog as a canine.
- easier: Have the entire household uphold a single boundary—off the bed—for one day.
- alt: A family sits at the dining table while the Dog rests in its bed
- caption: Consistent boundaries for all

### bed
- label: Whines at night outside my bed
- reassure: The initial nights outside your bed are challenging. Vocal protests are expected.
- cause: Used to co-sleeping, its own bed does not feel like a high-value sanctuary yet.
- c1: Position its bed in a cozy corner with an unwashed piece of your clothing.
- c2: If whining starts, do not pick up. Wait for settling; if checking welfare, enter silently and depart.
- easier: Nap in its own bed with your clothing scent, 2 times.
- alt: The Dog sleeps in its bed beside a piece of clothing
- caption: A dedicated bed with your scent

## general
- reassure: Resetting years of habits takes time. A rough day does not derail long-term progress.
- cause: Trying to eliminate every routine simultaneously is overwhelming.
- c1: Begin with a single boundary and introduce the next only once the first is automatic.
- c2: Review the "A Dog, Not a Toy" lesson.
- easier: Half a day without picking up the Dog.
- alt: The Dog rests calmly in its bed while the Handler reads
- caption: One boundary at a time
