# The Poste on the Stairwell

## target
When descending the stairs or exiting the lobby, perform a Poste every time the leash becomes Taut.

## success
Your Dog reorients toward you and the leash returns to Slack before moving forward.

## problems
### pulls
- label: Keeps pulling even after I stop
- reassure: Many Dogs pull harder when you first anchor. It is expected.
- cause: Testing whether increased pulling works, as it did in the past.
- c1: Hold the Poste without taking a single step until the leash returns to Slack.
- c2: Use the Marker the instant the leash goes Slack, even slightly.
- easier: Perform a Poste in the hallway at home until Slack returns, 3 times.
- alt: A Handler stands still while the Dog stops pulling
- caption: Stand still until Slack returns

### pulled
- label: I ended up pulling back
- reassure: Pulling back is an oppositional reflex. Almost everyone experiences it.
- cause: The leash was likely gathered too short or you were rushing.
- c1: Anchor your leash hand against your hip: this prevents arm-reeving.
- c2: Leave with plenty of time: the Poste technique requires patience.
- easier: Perform a Poste with hand on hip in the lobby, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler stands with the leash hand anchored firmly against the hip
- caption: Leash hand on hip

### long
- label: Took a very long time
- reassure: Taking time at first is a positive sign: the Dog is processing cause and effect.
- cause: The Dog does not yet understand what reinitiates forward motion.
- c1: Speak its name softly once to prompt a check-in head turn.
- c2: Practice in a low-distraction setting with minimal environmental interest.
- easier: Perform a Poste in a quiet hallway until a turn occurs, 3 times.
- alt: The Dog turns back toward the Handler in a quiet hallway
- caption: A low-distraction area

## general
- reassure: Building stairwells are high-arousal bottlenecks. Every bit of progress matters.
- cause: Crossing the doorway threshold represents the highest arousal point of the walk.
- c1: Establish a calm harness ritual before heading into the stairwell.
- c2: Pause on each stair landing even if no pulling occurs.
- easier: Perform a Poste only on the final flight of stairs, 2 times.
- alt: A Handler and Dog standing calmly on a building landing
- caption: Pause on every landing
