# Blue step

## intro
Your dog puts its front paws on a step and pivots around it following a treat. It is a coordination and balance exercise that builds strength and body awareness.

## materials
- A low, stable, non-slip step or platform
- Tiny treats

## steps
1. Place the step on a firm surface. Let your dog sniff and explore it.
2. With a treat in your hand, lure your dog to put one front paw on the step. "Good" and treat.
3. Repeat until it puts both front paws up. Reward every attempt, even a small one.
4. With the front paws up, slowly move the treat to one side so your dog swings its back legs around.
5. Reward after a quarter turn and build up little by little to a full circle.

## tips
- If your dog hesitates, use a lower step or reward just one step towards it.
- Keep sessions to a couple of minutes and let your dog rest between rounds.
- Never force or push your dog: if it is unsure, go back to the previous step.
