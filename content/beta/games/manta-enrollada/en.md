# Rolled blanket

## intro
Hide kibble inside a rolled-up blanket and let your dog find it by scent. It is a cheap sniffing game that tires the mind, and it takes a minute to set up with things you already have at home.

## materials
- An old, thick blanket or towel
- A handful of kibble from the daily ration

## steps
1. Spread the blanket on the floor and scatter the kibble along it, leaving one end strip without food.
2. Roll the blanket up like a spring roll, not too tight, so the smell comes out of the ends.
3. Put the roll on the floor and say "find it" in a calm voice. If your dog hesitates, bring its nose to one end.
4. Let your dog sniff and pull the kibble out at its own pace. Every piece found is its reward.
5. When it is done, take the blanket away before your dog starts chewing it.

## tips
- Start with a very loose roll and leave a couple of pieces visible at the tip, so your dog gets the idea.
- To make it harder, roll tighter or fold the roll in half.
- It is normal for your dog to use a paw or flip the roll over; let it try.

## safety
Always supervise your dog while it plays with the blanket. If it starts chewing or pulling threads, take it away: swallowed fabric can block the gut. Use a blanket much bigger than your dog's mouth, without fringes or loose threads, and put it away when the game ends.
