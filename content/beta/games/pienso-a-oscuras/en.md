# Kibble in the dark

## intro
With the lights off, your dog stops relying on sight and finds the kibble by scent alone. A calm game for the end of the day, ideal when it rains and there is no long walk.

## materials
- A handful of kibble from the daily ration
- A room you know well, with nothing hazardous on the floor

## steps
1. Clear the floor of the room and keep a dim light on while you set up.
2. Scatter the kibble on the floor, first in a short line with the pieces fairly close together.
3. Have your dog wait by your side and switch the light off. Say "find it" softly.
4. Stay still or in a corner while your dog sniffs. Do not guide it: every piece found is its reward.
5. When everything is picked up, turn the light on and give one last piece calmly.

## tips
- If it is hard, let some light in from the hallway; full darkness is not needed.
- After a few sessions, scatter the kibble further apart and in several areas of the room.
- If your dog seems uneasy in the dark, turn the light on and try again another day with a dim light.
