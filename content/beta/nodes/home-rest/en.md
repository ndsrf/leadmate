# World 1 · Resting at Home

## primer
### card 1
Remember: home is for resting. If the Dog asks for attention, do not look, speak, or touch; if it persists, walk calmly into its space.
- caption: Inside is for resting; outside is for playing

## checkpoint
### tq1
What does the Dog learn if you respond every time it demands attention?
- [x] That demanding works and it should demand more
- [ ] That it has had its share for the day
- [ ] That you decide when playtime happens
- why: Every response, even a "not now", rewards the demand, so the Dog demands more.

### tq2
The Dog comes nudging you with its nose. What do you do?
- [x] Do not look at it, speak to it, or touch it
- [ ] Say "not now" in a firm voice
- [ ] Stroke it briefly so it leaves
- why: Looking at, speaking to, or touching the Dog is already a response: give none of that.

### tq3
The Dog persists and does not leave. What do you do?
- [x] Walk calmly into its space
- [ ] Throw the ball down the hallway
- [ ] Give it a toy to keep it occupied
- why: If it persists, walk calmly into its space without touching until it moves off; playtime belongs outside.

## exercises
- timing: At home: when the Dog comes demanding attention, do not look at it, speak to it, or touch it.
- tension: At home: put away living room toys and play with them only outside.
