# World 6 · Keep It Fresh

## primer
### card 1
Remember: a change is fresh when you have not just made it. Vary turns, pace and stops, mark the look that follows, then walk on with a Slack leash or say "Go Sniff".
- caption: A fresh change, a look, then the reward

## checkpoint
### tq1
You have turned left three times in a row and {name} stopped looking up. What helps?
- [x] Try something different: a stop, a slow-down or a turn the other way
- [ ] Turn left again, faster
- [ ] Say {name}'s name louder at each turn
- why: Repeating one move makes it predictable. A different change is new again, so {name} has a reason to look.

### tq2
{name} followed your turn and glanced at you. What is the best next step?
- [x] Mark the look, then walk on Slack or say "Go Sniff"
- [ ] Turn again at once
- [ ] Wait until {name} looks a second time
- why: The mark on the look, then Slack walking or "Go Sniff", completes a Chain.

### tq3
Walking in a straight line on autopilot, you reach a smell. What do you do?
- [x] Change something first, then let {name} choose you before you give "Go Sniff"
- [ ] Walk past it quickly
- [ ] Let {name} pull you to it
- why: Autopilot makes smells win. A change brings {name} back to you, and then the smell is a reward.

## exercises
- tension: Outside: keep the leash Slack through every turn, pace change and stop.
- attention: Outside: change something every so often, vary the kind, and mark each look.
