# Walk with Distraction

## primer
### card 1
Remember: look ahead into the distance, use Poste if the leash goes Taut, and reward a Slack leash with "good" or by letting the Dog sniff.
- caption: Look far ahead: you spot it before the Dog does

### card 2
In the game: use the joystick to walk, release it to use Poste, and use Go Sniff as a reward.

## checkpoint
### tq1
You only say something to your Dog when it pulls. What happens?
- [x] Periods of Slack leash earn no reinforcement
- [ ] It learns that pulling achieves nothing
- [ ] It walks better because it senses you watching
- why: What the Dog does right before a reward is what it learns; if you only react to pulling, good moments pass without reinforcement.

### tq2
You spot a skateboard approaching in the distance. What do you do?
- [x] Step aside before the leash goes Taut
- [ ] Keep going the same way and use Poste if it lunges
- [ ] Speed up to pass it as fast as possible
- why: Looking far ahead lets you see it before your Dog does: step aside or slow down before the leash goes Taut. When in doubt, more distance.

### tq3
The Dog wants to sniff a tree. When do you let it?
- [x] When it arrives with the leash hanging Slack
- [ ] When it arrives, even if it pulled
- [ ] Never, because sniffing distracts from the walk
- why: If it arrives pulling, the scent rewards the pull; if it arrives with a Slack leash, the scent rewards the Slack leash.

## exercises
- tension: Outside: during a walk, count how many times the leash goes Taut and lower that number each day.
- timing: At home: reward with your voice right when your Dog looks away from a toy.
- poste: Outside: when you see something disturbing, stop like a Poste before the leash goes Taut.
- attention: At home: reward each time your Dog looks at you without being called.
