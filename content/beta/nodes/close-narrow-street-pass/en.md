# World 4 · Narrow Pass with Distractor

## primer
### card 1
Remember: on narrow sidewalks, use Leash Gathering calmly and use Handler Shielding between your Dog and the oncoming dog.
- caption: Handler Shielding position and Leash Gathering in a narrow pass

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why do you position yourself between your Dog and the approaching dog?
- [x] To break the line of sight and prevent Arousal from rising
- [ ] To force the dogs to greet each other
- [ ] To pull hard if the Dog tries to run
- why: Handler Shielding blocks direct Fixation and gives your Dog space and calm before reactivity escalates.

### tq2
How should you shorten the leash length on a narrow sidewalk?
- [x] By using Leash Gathering gently, maintaining a relaxed Slack curve
- [ ] By giving a sharp yank backward
- [ ] By wrapping it around your hand until it is completely rigid
- why: Leash Gathering gently keeps the leash functional without transmitting a Rigid Pull that alerts the Dog.

### tq3
When is the best moment to use Poste and reward calm?
- [x] Use Poste if there is a sudden pull, and reward right when the Dog looks away from the other dog
- [ ] Reward when the Dog is already barking or pulling
- [ ] Use Poste the entire time without releasing Slack
- why: Using Poste absorbs force without jerking, and the Marker reinforces the exact instant your Dog decides to break Fixation.

## exercises
- tension: Outside: practice gathering half a meter of leash with both hands without sharp pulls.
- timing: On the walk: use Handler Shielding between your Dog and an oncoming Distractor.
- attention: In the park: use the Marker and reward each time your Dog looks at another dog and looks back at you.
