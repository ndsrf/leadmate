# World 4 · Scooters and Parked Cars

## primer
### card 1
Remember: if a scooter approaches, step behind the nearest parked car before the Dog starts fixating.
- caption: Behind the car, {name} looks at you

## checkpoint
### tq1
When do you step behind the parked car?
- [x] Before the Dog starts fixating on the scooter
- [ ] When the Dog starts barking at the scooter
- [ ] When the scooter is already right beside you
- why: Fixation is the first sign: if you act before, the car breaks that gaze and tension does not escalate.

### tq2
A fast scooter is coming. What Visual Shield do you use?
- [x] The nearest parked car
- [ ] A doorway at the end of the street
- [ ] The lamp post right next to you
- why: With a fast-moving Distractor like a scooter, use the closest Visual Shield that truly blocks line of sight.

### tq3
Behind the car, {name} looks at you. What do you do?
- [x] Say "good" and give a treat
- [ ] Step back out now before the scooter passes
- [ ] Let the Dog peek out to see the scooter
- why: Looking at you behind the shield is what you want: reinforce it with a gentle "good" and give a treat.

## exercises
- timing: Outside: when you see something fast, call your Dog before it fixates.
- poste: Outside: place a parked car between your Dog and whatever disturbs it.
- attention: At home: practice both Cues with different toys.
