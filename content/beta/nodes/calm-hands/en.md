# World 1 · Calm Hands and Leash

## primer
### card 1
Remember: your Dog is a dog, not a toy. If a nearby hand excites it, do not bring it closer to its mouth: gather the leash calmly and wait for it to relax.
- caption: On the ground, at its eye level, a hand is a toy

### card 2
In the game, hold Gather so it cannot reach the hand, use Poste if it persists, and tap Marker when calm: your Dog receives a reward.

## checkpoint
### tq1
Your Dog gets excited with a hand nearby. What do you do first?
- [x] Gather the leash calmly so it cannot reach the hand
- [ ] Bring the hand closer so it can sniff it
- [ ] Speak loudly until it stops
- why: Gathering the leash keeps the hand out of reach without frustration: it relaxes sooner and hands stop being a toy.

### tq2
When do you use the Marker?
- [x] When calm, right before rewarding
- [ ] When it lunges toward the hand
- [ ] When the hand is already in its mouth
- why: The Marker signals "that is what I want": it only belongs in the exact instant of calm.

### tq3
The Dog lunges for the hand but falls short. What do you do?
- [x] Gather the leash again and wait for it to calm down
- [ ] Restart the exercise angrily
- [ ] Give a treat to calm it down
- why: A missed attempt is no problem: gather, wait, and use the Marker when calm returns. The reward only comes with calm.

## exercises
- tension: At home: on leash, offer a hand and gather the leash if it lunges. Use the Marker for calm and give a reward.
- timing: At home: practice for one minute daily; if it gets too excited, pause and resume calmly.

## debrief
- calm: It calmed down with the hand nearby! That is how it is taught: gather, wait, use the Marker, and reward.
- notCalm: Not calm yet, which is completely normal at first. Tip: gather the leash before excitement peaks and wait without speaking.
