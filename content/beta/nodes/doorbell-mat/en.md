# World 2 · The Doorbell Mat

## primer
### card 1
Remember: when the doorbell rings, go to the mat; use the Marker and reward only when the Dog lies down relaxed.
- caption: The doorbell rings and {name} lies down on the mat

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why does your Dog run to the door upon hearing the doorbell?
- [x] The doorbell announces something exciting
- [ ] It wants to alert you to open quickly
- [ ] The high-pitched doorbell sound bothers it
- why: The doorbell almost always announces a visitor or package: it is excitement and habit, not disobedience.

### tq2
The doorbell rings and the Dog stares fixedly at the door. What do you do?
- [x] Give the Cue "go to mat" once
- [ ] Wait to see if it calms down on its own
- [ ] Give a treat to distract it
- why: As soon as it rings, or as soon as the Dog fixates on the door or barks, give the Cue "go to mat" once, before Arousal spikes.

### tq3
The Dog is on the mat, standing and whining. What do you do?
- [x] Wait without speaking for it to lie down
- [ ] Give a treat for having reached the mat
- [ ] Repeat "go to mat" louder
- why: If you give a treat while the Dog is still restless, you reward the excitement; the spoken Marker word "good" comes when it lies down and releases its body.

## exercises
- timing: At home: have someone ring the doorbell and reward your Dog right as it calms on its mat.
- attention: At home: when the doorbell rings, wait for your Dog to look at you before opening the door.
