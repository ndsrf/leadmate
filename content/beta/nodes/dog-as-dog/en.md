# World 1 · A Dog, Not a Plush Toy

## primer
### card 1
Remember: on the floor, in its bed, and no kisses on the face. Greet the Dog when it is calm.
- caption: On the floor, at its level, it is a dog

### card 2
In the game, observe its state at each step and tap the action that helps: ignore, to bed, or use the Marker. Smothering it with affection never helps.

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why is picking up the Dog in your arms not advisable?
- [x] It loses control of its own body
- [ ] It will not want to walk outside afterward
- [ ] Only puppies need that
- why: For a dog, being lifted off the ground means losing control of its body: many tolerate it, few enjoy it.

### tq2
The Dog gets on the couch. What do you do?
- [x] Walk calmly into its space without touching it
- [ ] Leave it, as it is more comfortable that way
- [ ] Show a treat to lure it down
- why: Walk calmly into its space without touching or speaking to it until it steps down; then point to its bed.

### tq3
When do you greet the Dog upon arriving home?
- [x] When all four paws are on the floor
- [ ] As soon as it jumps, to calm it down
- [ ] When it reaches up with its paws to be held
- why: Greet the Dog only when it is calm: if it jumps, wait without speaking; once all four paws are on the floor, greet slowly.

## exercises
- timing: At home: when the Dog asks to be picked up, wait without looking until all four paws are on the floor.
- tension: At home: if the Dog gets on the couch, walk calmly into its space without touching it until it steps down and goes to its bed.
