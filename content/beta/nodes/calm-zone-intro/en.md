## checkpoint
### tq1
What is your Dog's reactivity threshold?
- [x] The distance beyond which it only reacts
- [ ] The time it holds out without barking at another dog
- [ ] The moment it has gotten used to something
- why: The threshold is the distance point from a Distractor beyond which the Dog can no longer think and only reacts.

### tq2
You see a dog and there is no Visual Shield nearby. What do you do?
- [x] Walk on its side using Handler Shielding to block its view
- [ ] Stop so it gets used to looking at it
- [ ] Walk straight and fast to pass as quickly as possible
- why: If there is no Visual Shield, use Handler Shielding: walk between the Dog and the Distractor so your body blocks its view.

### tq3
Behind the Visual Shield, your Dog refuses food. What do you do?
- [x] Increase distance: switch sidewalks or turn around
- [ ] Insist with the treat until it eats
- [ ] Wait right there until it passes on its own
- why: If it cannot eat, it is still over threshold: increase distance. When in doubt, more distance.
