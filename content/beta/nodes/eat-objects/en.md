# At Home · Eating Non-Food Objects

## primer
### card 1
Remember: if the Dog swallowed it, go to the vet. If not, put away what it usually grabs, do not chase, and trade the object for a higher-value treat.
- caption: A higher-value treat gets the Dog to release the object

## checkpoint
### tq1
What do you do if the Dog swallowed something?
- [x] Call the veterinarian immediately
- [ ] Wait to see if it passes it
- [ ] Try to induce vomiting at home
- why: A swallowed object can be an emergency: call your veterinarian right away.

### tq2
The Dog runs with a sock. What do you do?
- [x] Do not chase and offer a treat instead
- [ ] Run after it to take it away
- [ ] Yell to drop it now
- why: Chasing turns it into a game; offering a higher-value treat makes it drop the item.

### tq3
What is most effective in preventing swallowing non-food items?
- [x] Putting away items it usually grabs
- [ ] Scolding the Dog when it grabs something
- [ ] Letting it taste it so it learns
- why: Prevention is safer than training: what the Dog cannot reach, it cannot swallow.

## exercises
- timing: At home: put away five items at the Dog's eye level that it usually grabs.
- tension: At home: practice the trade with a toy and a higher-value treat.
