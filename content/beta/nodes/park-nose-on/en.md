# World 6 · Let Them Sniff

## primer
### card 1
Remember: wait for {name} to look at you, mark it, then say "Go Sniff" and let {name} read the smell with a Slack leash. Say "Let's Go" to set off together again.
- caption: A look first, then the smell

## checkpoint
### tq1
{name} slows down at a bush, nose low, and glances at you. What do you do?
- [x] Mark the glance, then say "Go Sniff" and wait
- [ ] Say "Let's Go" and walk on to keep the pace
- [ ] Pull gently so {name} does not stop
- why: The glance is a Check-in. Marking it and giving "Go Sniff" makes the smell the reward.

### tq2
While {name} sniffs, where should the leash be?
- [x] Slack, hanging in a U
- [ ] Taut, so {name} cannot wander
- [ ] Wrapped around your hand
- why: A Slack leash lets {name} read the smell calmly and keeps the walk pleasant for both.

### tq3
What does {name} learn if pulling toward a smell gets there faster?
- [x] That pulling works
- [ ] That smells are not important
- [ ] That you are in charge of every smell
- why: Smells reached by pulling teach pulling. Smells reached after a look teach looking.
