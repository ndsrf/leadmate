# World 3 · The Blind Corner

## primer
### card 1
Remember: slow down a few steps before the corner, gather the Dog to your side, and take the wide arc around the turn.
- caption: On the wide side of the sidewalk, you see whatever appears ahead

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why is what appears around a corner so startling?
- [x] Because it appears suddenly and very close
- [ ] Because the Dog does not know that street well
- [ ] Because the Dog has never seen a bicycle
- why: What startles is proximity and suddenness: distance gives time, and a blind corner erases it.

### tq2
When do you start slowing down before a blind corner?
- [x] Three or four steps before reaching it
- [ ] Right as you reach the corner
- [ ] Once you can already see the other street
- why: At the corner itself it is already too late: slowing down three or four steps before gives the Dog time to reach your side.

### tq3
How do you turn a blind corner with your Dog?
- [x] Along the wide arc, with the Dog by your side
- [ ] Hugging the wall, with the Dog by your side
- [ ] Along the wide arc, with the Dog in front
- why: Taking the wide arc with the Dog at your side lets you see the other street before it does, keeping whatever comes a few meters away.

## exercises
- poste: Outside: before each corner, slow down and let your Dog move to your side.
- tension: Outside: turn corners slowly and count how many times the leash goes Taut.
- timing: At home: reward with your voice right when your Dog looks away from something concerning.
