# World 5 · The Runner

## primer
### card 1
Remember: when calm, give the full three meters; if something fast appears, gather the leash before the Dog accelerates and move aside.
- caption: When calm, three meters; if something fast arrives, one

### card 2
In the game, the long leash only goes Taut at the end: when it straightens, release the joystick.

## checkpoint
### tq1
Why does a long leash require anticipating ahead?
- [x] Over three meters the Dog builds up a lot of speed
- [ ] The pull at the end teaches it not to run
- [ ] With more leash it gets tangled around your legs
- why: Three meters is a runway: the Dog hits the end at full speed and the sudden halt jolts you both without teaching anything useful.

### tq2
You see a runner in the distance. What do you do first?
- [x] Gather the leash before the Dog accelerates
- [ ] Wait to see if {name} notices or not
- [ ] Give all the leash so it does not pull
- why: As soon as you see something fast, gather the leash fold by fold before the Dog bolts: with less leash, it builds up less speed.

### tq3
How do you hold the excess length of a long leash?
- [x] In loose folds inside your palm
- [ ] Wrapped around your hand so it does not slip
- [ ] Wrapped around your fingers to shorten quickly
- why: Wrapped around your hand or fingers, the loop tightens dangerously if the Dog bolts; loose folds can be gathered and released in a second.

## exercises
- poste: In the park: when you see someone running, stop before the leash goes Taut.
- tension: In the park: with a long leash, gather it slightly before passing people.
