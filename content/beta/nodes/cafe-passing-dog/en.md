# World 7 · Another Dog Passes Your Table

## primer
### card 1
Remember: on the terrace, gather the leash short but slack, put {name} on the wall side and stand between them and the passing dog. Mark when they look back.
- caption: Short slack leash, with you between {name} and the passing dog

## checkpoint
### tq1
Another dog walks past your table and {name} stares at it. What do you do first?
- [x] Gather the leash calmly and stand between {name} and the dog
- [ ] Leave the leash long so {name} moves away alone
- [ ] Pull the leash back so {name} stops looking
- why: Gathering calmly and acting as a shield takes the other dog out of view without tensing the leash. If it is too much, you can always change tables or leave.

### tq2
Another dog passes along the walkway. Which leash helps most?
- [x] A short slack leash, with {name} on the far side of the chair
- [ ] A long leash across the walkway, with {name} near the passage
- why: Short and slack keeps {name} by the wall and out of the passage. Long and across the walkway brings them closer to the other dog and costs you control.

### tq3
{name} looks at the passing dog and then looks back at you. What do you do?
- [x] Say "good" and give a treat
- [ ] Scold them for looking at the other dog
- [ ] Wait until they are fully calm before rewarding
- why: Looking back at you after seeing the other dog is their own decision. Marking it at that moment makes them repeat it.

## debrief
- calm: {name} stayed calm as the other dog passed! That is how it is done: short slack leash, your body as a shield and "good" when they look back.
- notCalm: It was still hard, and that is normal at first. Tip: gather earlier, stand between the two and, if it is too much, change tables or leave.

## exercises
- tension: At home: practise gathering half a metre of slack leash with both hands, without jerking.
- timing: On the terrace: stand between {name} and the passage when you see someone coming.
- attention: On the terrace: mark and reward each time {name} looks at whoever passes and then looks back at you.
