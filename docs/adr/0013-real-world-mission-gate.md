# Progress is gated by real-world Missions, not a daily quota

Each Stretch of a World ends in a Mission: one concrete real-world target. The user checks in on it no earlier than the next calendar day and at least 8 hours after starting it. No Stop after the Mission opens until it is passed. That gate is the only limit on daily progress. Within an open Stretch the user can do as much as they like, and finished Stops, the Daily Stage and the Theory Library always stay open for practice. We chose this because LeadMate exists to change real walks. A user who finishes the simulator in one sitting has trained their thumb, not their dog. Tying the wait to a real walk makes the wait mean something.

## Considered options

- **A daily quota of N Stops:** simple, but it stops the user at an arbitrary point, sometimes halfway through theory, and the wait has nothing to do with practising on a real walk. Rejected.
- **No gate, with a same-day "how did it go?" on the Debrief (the previous behaviour):** asked before any walk could have happened, so the answers carried no information. Rejected.
- **Next calendar day only:** consistent with the Streak, but a Mission started at 23:50 could be checked in at 00:10. Rejected in favour of next calendar day **and** at least 8 hours.
- **A strict gate with no way out until "¡Conseguido!":** some dogs would keep users stuck for weeks, and they would leave. Rejected. From the second "Costó un poco" on the same Mission, the user may move on anyway, and the Mission keeps a "keep practising" mark.

## Consequences

- A "Costó un poco" check-in shows a Fix: reassurance, the likely cause, what to change, an easier target and what to practise again. The Mission is reassigned, and the wait starts over. Passing the easier target passes the Mission.
- After three "Hoy no pudimos" check-ins in a row, the app offers the easier target.
- Records created before Missions existed keep their progress. A Mission that was never started counts as passed when a Node after it in its World is already completed. A World's last Mission has no Node after it, so on migration the record keeps a one-time copy of the Nodes it had completed, and that Mission counts as passed when every Node of its Stretch is in that copy. Returning users are never locked out of content they had already reached, and users who started with Missions never get a Mission for free.
- The gate only adds friction; it enforces nothing. A user can tap "¡Conseguido!" without walking, and the next Stretch's simulation still trains the skill.
- Check-in reminders (notifications) are left for a later change. For now the waiting Mission on the World Map and the Streak do the reminding.
