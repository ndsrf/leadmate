# World 1 is composed per Dog from its Concerns

The Home Stretches (biting the owner, biting other people, barking at home, destroying things, eating non-food) were first planned outside every World, in a Home Behaviours section, so they gated nothing. Users then could not find them. A Home Stretch is now a Stretch of World 1 while the Dog has that Concern, placed by the Personal Plan (biting first) and never before the Stretch that holds a prerequisite Node of its Nodes. The ones the Dog does not have sit in a collapsed, optional section between World 1 and World 2 that gates nothing and counts toward no total. World 1 therefore completes only when the Dog's own Home Stretches are done, and `WORLDS` is no longer the whole truth for World 1: it is the Basics plus the Dog's Home Stretches.

This supersedes the rule in `add-home-behaviour-training` that Home Stretches count in no World and have no Day Skip.

## Considered Options

- **Keep them outside the Worlds (before):** never blocks the trail, but users do not see them.
- **Always in World 1:** simple, but a Dog with no biting would have to pass a biting Mission to reach World 2.
- **Declared ones in World 1, the rest optional (chosen):** the user's own problems are on the trail; the rest stay discoverable.

## Consequences

World completion depends on the Concerns. To keep progress from moving backwards, a Home Stretch declared after World 1 is complete stays optional, and a started one stays in World 1 if the Concern is removed. Home Missions are normal Missions, with check-in, "Hoy no pudimos", Day Skip and Owed. World 2 and the optional section are folded by default.

World 1 for a Dog is ordered: biting Home Stretches, the Basics Stretch that holds the how-to-play Lesson (`howto-choose-action`, now the first Stop of World 1), the other Home Stretches in Personal Plan order (by level), then the other Basics Stretches. Every read-the-Dog Node (the Basics and Home Nodes) needs that Lesson read, so a Stretch with such a Node never sits before the Stretch holding it; a Home Node played from Other Home Behaviours is off the trail and does not ask for it. The Lessons of a Stretch are open together (a Lesson is closed only by an unpassed earlier Mission or a locked World); a Node needs all the Lessons of its Stretch read and a Mission needs the Node.
